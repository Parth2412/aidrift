# AIDrift Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in AIDrift free from harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or technology choices.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- showing empathy and respect for different experiences and viewpoints;
- giving and accepting constructive, specific feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning when mistakes affect others;
- focusing on what is best for users and the community; and
- protecting credentials, personal information, private prompts/model outputs, and customer data.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, trolling, personal attacks, publishing another person's private information without permission, or any conduct that would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, release/beta channels, community events, and when someone officially represents AIDrift.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Enforcement decisions should explain their rationale while protecting reporter privacy.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a private contact method listed on the [`@Parth2412` GitHub profile](https://github.com/Parth2412). Do not put sensitive conduct reports into a public issue. If no private maintainer channel is available, public beta recruitment must remain paused until the owner establishes one; violations of GitHub's policies can also be reported through [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-content).

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. A maintainer with a conflict of interest must recuse themselves.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will consider context, impact, and recurrence when choosing a response:

1. **Correction:** A private warning and explanation for inappropriate language or behavior.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning with boundaries and consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary restriction:** A time-limited ban from interaction or representation.
4. **Permanent restriction:** Permanent removal for sustained violations, harassment, or threats.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
